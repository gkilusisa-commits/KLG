Who we are

A legacy is built

Kilusisa Legacy Group is a construction and mining services company built to take on demanding work and deliver it with care. We support projects from the ground up with practical expertise, capable equipment and a commitment to doing the job right.

Our work spans earthworks, civil construction, demolition and mining services. Across every site, we bring a hands-on mindset, clear communication and respect for the people and places connected to the project.
